What role did the Church play in medieval medicine? :: Controlled universities and training; promoted Galen; ran hospitals; encouraged prayer and religious explanations; restricted dissection

Name the four humours and the organ/element/season each was linked to. :: Blood (heart, air, spring); phlegm (brain, water, winter); yellow bile (liver, fire, summer); black bile (spleen, earth, autumn)
Who provided most medical care in medieval England? :: Women in the home (mothers, wise women) using herbal remedies – care was cheapest and most common at home
Why did most medieval hospitals not admit people with infectious diseases? :: Feared spreading infection (miasma) and they focused on care and the soul, not cure
What new approach to hospital care developed in the 13th century? :: More hospitals founded by the Church and wealthy donors, some specialising (e.g. leper hospitals); emphasis on cleanliness, rest and prayer for the soul
What actually caused the Black Death? :: Bacteria (Yersinia pestis) spread by fleas on rats (bubonic) and by coughing (pneumonic) – unknown at the time
